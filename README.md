# ESG-Financial-Performance-Dashboard-on-Tableau-Public
The Tableau story attempts to assess the relationship between ESG, corporate governance, and financial performance across nine markets in the Asia-Pacific (APAC) region from 2010 to 2024. The story consists of three dashboards with three different purposes.

## Interactive Dashboard

You can access the live, interactive Tableau Dashboard here: 
https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/steven.yeung3151/viz/13700791_Yeung_Viz/TableauStory

Dashboard 1 - Asia-Pacific Market Landscape & ESG Trends (2010–2024): Com-panies in most countries recorded improved ESG scores over the years, and South Korea outperformed peers in other countries. However, ESG performance across countries varied due to differences in industry composition.
